Plunk identity

Your camera. Your folders. Plunk.

Signature: Pic → Name → Location. Developer-facing support: Real-world context for your models.

Plunk is a useful little verb. It gets a picture from the world into your work, with as little ceremony as possible. The brand is playful; the file transfer is dependable. No AI magic, automatic model ingestion, or background-delivery claims.

Dark text on orange buttons. Rounded system typography; bold, tightly spaced lowercase wordmark with an orange period. public/brand/icon.svg and wordmark.svg are original editable vector masters. scripts/assets.mjs produces PNG app icons and the social graphic. System-font rendering can vary; convert the wordmark text to outlines in a design tool before handing it to a print vendor.

Use space generously. Let the photograph lead. One primary action per app screen. A settling animation follows a server-confirmed save; reduced motion disables it. No sound by default.

Voice

Take a pic. Name it. Choose a server. Plunk here. Plunked.

Use plain language during failures. Explain how to recover without jokes. Never label an upload successful before the listener confirms it. Setup details belong in settings and the guide, not the main workflow.
