Plunk launch kit

Positioning

For people whose work lives in project folders but whose context lives out in the world, Plunk is a camera-to-server utility that makes sending a named picture feel like three small steps. Start with developers using visual model context; keep the core identity broad enough for sketches, lab observations, repairs, and everyday documentation.

Short post

Meet Plunk.

Take a pic. Name it. Pick a folder.

Straight from your iPhone to your Linux server.

Built for the moment you want to tell your model: “Here, look.”

Long post

Your context isn’t all on your computer.

It’s on the whiteboard. Under the desk. Scribbled on the back of something.

Plunk gives those pictures somewhere to go: your project folders.

Take a picture. Give it a useful name. Tap a folder tile. With one saved server, Plunk takes you straight to its folders; with several, choose a server first. Plunk saves a JPEG there. No emailing yourself, no hunting for IMG_4827, no extra place to store the picture.

There’s a little setup first: run a listener on Linux, give it an HTTPS address, and save it on your phone. Each server chooses its authentication. After that, it’s Pic → Name → Location.

If you work with models, Plunk puts visual context where your tooling can use it. It doesn’t attach the file to a model automatically. It just gets it right where it belongs.

Your camera. Your folders. Plunk.

Release checklist

Initial name discovery on October 5, 2026 found an existing developer-facing Plunk email platform and its documentation. This is a meaningful product-name collision, especially for a developer audience. The current project retains Plunk as its working name; no trademark clearance or domain availability is claimed. The useplunk.com domain is already in use. A formal trademark search and selection of an available, distinguishable public identity remain release prerequisites.

Keep this kit local until the product is ready. Verify physical iPhone Safari and Home Screen capture, local trusted HTTPS, and an authenticated internet endpoint. Check name conflicts, likely domains, and trademark records in the intended market before public release. This kit does not represent legal clearance or a domain purchase.
